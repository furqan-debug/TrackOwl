# Download screenshots

> [!NOTE]
> Screenshots cannot be deleted from TrackOwl. They are kept as the record of a tracked session. What you can do is select the ones you need and download them.

## Overview
Every capture on the Screenshots page can be selected. Once you have a selection, TrackOwl packs those captures into a single ZIP file and downloads it, named after the member and the date.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Plan:** This is a Premium feature. On the Basic plan the page redirects to Pricing.
>
> **Who can do this:** Every role can open this page. Members see their own captures; **Owner**, **Admin**, **Manager** and **Viewer** accounts can also see the rest of the team.

## Where to find it
1. Open the TrackOwl dashboard and sign in.
2. In the left sidebar, open **Activity**.
3. Select **Screenshots** (`/dashboard/activity`).

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Pick the member and the date you want captures for.
2. Click a capture to select it. Selected captures are marked.
3. Keep selecting, up to 50 captures at a time. That is the batch limit, and TrackOwl tells you when you reach it.
4. Start the download. TrackOwl fetches each capture, packs them into a ZIP and saves it to your computer.

---

## Best Practices
> [!TIP]
> - Download by day. One member's day fits comfortably inside the 50-capture limit.
> - If you need captures as evidence, download them before they age out of your retention window.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I delete a screenshot?**
A: No. Screenshots are a permanent part of the tracked session record. If you need captures removed entirely, that is a data deletion request: email contact@trackowl.io.

**Q: Can I stop screenshots being readable instead?**
A: Yes. Turn on **Privacy Masking** under **Settings → Org Settings → Monitoring** and every capture is blurred.

**Q: Can I do this from the desktop app?**
A: No. The desktop app takes the captures; viewing and downloading them is done in the dashboard in your browser.
