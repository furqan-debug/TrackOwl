# Daily totals report

## Overview
The daily totals view breaks tracked time down day by day, rather than as one figure for the whole range. It is the view to use when you want to see the shape of a week.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Plan:** This is a Premium feature. On the Basic plan the page redirects to Pricing.
>
> **Who can do this:** Every role can open this page. Members see their own data; **Owner**, **Admin**, **Manager** and **Viewer** accounts can also see the rest of the team.

## Where to find it
1. Open the TrackOwl dashboard and sign in.
2. In the left sidebar, open **Reports**.
3. The daily totals view sits at `/dashboard/reports/daily`.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Open **Reports** in the left sidebar.
2. Choose the date range you are interested in.
3. Filter by team or by member if you want a narrower view.
4. Switch to the daily totals view to see the same range split by day.

---

## Best Practices
> [!TIP]
> - Compare a full week at a time; single days rarely tell you much on their own.
> - Filter to one team first. Whole-organisation totals hide the detail you are usually looking for.
