# Location tracking policies

> [!NOTE]
> Hello and welcome! If you are a beginner, don't worry. TrackOwl is designed to be very intuitive. This step-by-step guide covers Location tracking policies in TrackOwl.

## Overview
Understanding how to use the **Location tracking policies** feature is essential for maximizing operational visibility and team productivity. This tool has been engineered to provide detailed insights while remaining incredibly user-friendly.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Who can do this:** Owner, Admin, Manager, Viewer and Client accounts can open job sites. The User role cannot.

## Where to find it
1. Open the TrackOwl dashboard and sign in.
2. Go to `/dashboard/locations/job-sites`. Each job site carries its own radius, which is the area it covers.

There is no separate location policy screen — the radius on each site is the policy.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

Follow these steps to configure your settings properly:

1. Make sure you are logged into your TrackOwl workspace.
2. Open a job site and set its address and radius.
3. Find and click on the specific item you want to work on.
4. Make your desired changes on the screen.
5. Always remember to click **Save** or **Update** at the bottom so you don't lose your work!

> - Review your changes carefully before saving to prevent disruption to your team.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I undo this action once it is saved?**  
A: Most things can be changed back. Archiving a project is reversible. Deleting is not — a deleted project, team or to-do is gone. Screenshots cannot be deleted at all.

**Q: Can I do this from the desktop app?**  
A: The desktop app tracks time, activity and screenshots. Everything else — settings, people, projects, reports and billing — is done in the dashboard in your browser. TrackOwl does not have a mobile app.
