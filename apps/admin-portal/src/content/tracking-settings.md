# Tracking settings

> [!NOTE]
> Hello and welcome! If you are a beginner, don't worry. TrackOwl is designed to be very intuitive. This step-by-step guide covers Tracking settings in TrackOwl.

## Overview
Understanding how to use the **Tracking settings** feature is essential for maximizing operational visibility and team productivity. This tool has been engineered to provide detailed insights while remaining incredibly user-friendly.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Who can do this:** Only Owner and Admin accounts can change organisation settings.

## Where to find it
1. Open the TrackOwl dashboard and sign in.
2. In the left sidebar, open **Settings**, then **Org Settings**.
3. Open the **Telemetry** tab: App Usage Tracking, URL Tracking, Idle Detection, Auto-Terminate Session and its grace period.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

Follow these steps to configure your settings properly:

1. Make sure you are logged into your TrackOwl workspace.
2. Open **Settings** in the left sidebar, then **Org Settings**.
3. Switch App Usage Tracking, URL Tracking, Idle Detection or Auto-Terminate Session on or off.
4. Click **Save** in the page header.

---

## Best Practices
> [!TIP]
> - Change one thing at a time, then check the result before moving on.
> - Review your changes carefully before saving to prevent disruption to your team.
> - Reports pull the same data together across members and dates. Reports needs the Premium plan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I undo this action once it is saved?**  
A: Most things can be changed back. Archiving a project is reversible. Deleting is not — a deleted project, team or to-do is gone. Screenshots cannot be deleted at all.

**Q: Can I do this from the desktop app?**  
A: The desktop app tracks time, activity and screenshots. Everything else — settings, people, projects, reports and billing — is done in the dashboard in your browser. TrackOwl does not have a mobile app.
