# View current plan

> [!NOTE]
> Hello and welcome! If you are a beginner, don't worry. TrackOwl is designed to be very intuitive. This step-by-step guide covers View current plan in TrackOwl.

## Overview
Understanding how to use the **View current plan** feature is essential for maximizing operational visibility and team productivity. This tool has been engineered to provide detailed insights while remaining incredibly user-friendly.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Who can do this:** Billing is Owner-only. Other roles do not see Billing & Plans in the sidebar.

## Where to find it
1. Open the TrackOwl dashboard and sign in.
2. In the left sidebar, open **Settings**.
3. Select **Billing & Plans** (`/dashboard/settings/billing`).

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

Follow these steps to configure your settings properly:

1. Make sure you are logged into your TrackOwl workspace.
2. Open **Settings** in the left sidebar, then **Billing & Plans**.
3. Find and click on the specific item you want to work on.
4. Make your desired changes on the screen.
5. Always remember to click **Save** or **Update** at the bottom so you don't lose your work!

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does this affect my current billing cycle?**  
A: Changes to user counts or premium features will be prorated on your next invoice. Settings changes are completely free.

**Q: In what currencies are the amounts owed displayed?**  
A: Amounts are displayed in your Organization's default currency, which can be configured in General Settings.
