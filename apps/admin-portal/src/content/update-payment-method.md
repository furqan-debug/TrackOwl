# Update payment method

> [!NOTE]
> Hello and welcome! If you are a beginner, don't worry. TrackOwl is designed to be very intuitive. This step-by-step guide covers Update payment method in TrackOwl.

## Overview
Understanding how to use the **Update payment method** feature is essential for maximizing operational visibility and team productivity. This tool has been engineered to provide detailed insights while remaining incredibly user-friendly.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Who can do this:** Billing is **Owner**-only. Other roles do not see Billing & Plans in the sidebar.

## Where to find it
1. Open the TrackOwl dashboard and sign in.
2. In the left sidebar, open **Settings**.
3. Select **Billing & Plans** (`/dashboard/settings/billing`).

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

Follow these steps to configure your settings properly:

1. Make sure you are logged into your TrackOwl workspace.
2. Open **Settings** in the left sidebar, then **Billing & Plans**.
3. Choose **Manage Subscription**. This opens the payment provider's billing portal in a new page.
4. Update the card or billing details there. TrackOwl does not hold your card details itself, so there is no Save button here.

---

## Best Practices
> [!TIP]
> - Regularly export your financial reports at the end of each month for accounting.
> - Double-check hourly rates before generating final payment reports.
> - Keep your payment methods up to date to avoid subscription interruptions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does this affect my current billing cycle?**  
A: Changes to user counts or premium features will be prorated on your next invoice. Settings changes are completely free.

**Q: In what currencies are the amounts owed displayed?**  
A: Amounts are displayed in your Organization's default currency, which can be configured in General Settings.
