# Open a capture full size

## Overview
Clicking a capture opens it full size over the page. The viewer is for reading a single moment properly and for stepping through the day without going back to the grid each time.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Plan:** This is a Premium feature. On the Basic plan the page redirects to Pricing.
>
> **Who can do this:** Every role can open their own captures. **Owner**, **Admin**, **Manager** and **Viewer** accounts can open anyone's.

## Where to find it
1. Open the TrackOwl dashboard and sign in.
2. In the left sidebar, open **Activity**, then **Screenshots**.
3. Click any capture in the grid.

---

## What the viewer shows
Along the top you get the member's name, the activity score for that capture, and the time it was taken.

| Action | How |
| --- | --- |
| Next or previous capture | The arrows either side of the image |
| Close the viewer | The close button, or the **Escape** key |

---

## Best Practices
> [!TIP]
> - Use the arrows to walk through a stretch of the day in order; it reads far better than opening captures one at a time.
> - The activity score on a capture is for that moment, not the whole session. A low one on its own means very little.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I download the capture I am looking at?**
A: Downloading is done from the grid, where you select the captures you want and download them together as a ZIP.

**Q: Can I edit or hide part of a capture?**
A: No. The only option is Privacy Masking, which blurs every capture for the whole organisation.
