# Archive a project

## Overview
Archiving takes a project out of the active list while keeping everything tracked against it. Use it when work finishes but you still want the history. Archiving can be undone — an archived project can be made active again.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Who can do this:** Owner, Admin and Manager accounts can archive a project. Viewer accounts can see projects but not change them.

## Where to find it
1. Open the TrackOwl dashboard and sign in.
2. In the left sidebar, open **Projects**.
3. Select **Projects** (`/dashboard/projects`).

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Find the project in the list.
2. Open its menu and choose **Archive**. Its status changes from Active to Archived.
3. To bring it back, open the menu again and set it back to Active.
4. To archive several at once, select them in the list and archive the selection together.

---

## Best Practices
> [!TIP]
> - Archive rather than delete. Deleting a project loses the record; archiving keeps it.
> - Archive finished client work so your active list stays short and your budgets stay meaningful.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I archive the whole organisation?**
A: No. Archiving applies to projects. If you are closing an account down, email contact@trackowl.io.

**Q: Does archiving delete the tracked time?**
A: No. Everything tracked against the project is kept, and still appears in reports for past dates.

**Q: Can I do this from the desktop app?**
A: No. Projects are managed in the dashboard in your browser.
