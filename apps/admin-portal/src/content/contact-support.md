# Contact Support

If you need help with TrackOwl, our support team is ready to assist you. You can reach us through any of the channels below.

## Email Support

For account issues, billing questions, technical problems, or any other inquiries, email us directly:

**[contact@trackowl.io](mailto:contact@trackowl.io)**

You can also use the contact form at the bottom of the Help Center. Average response time is under 2 hours on business days.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is the desktop app asking for Screen Recording permission?
On macOS, TrackOwl uses the system's built-in screen capture to take periodic screenshots during tracked sessions, and macOS requires explicit permission for that. Grant it in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording**. Windows does not ask for this.

### The app shows a "Screen Recording Permission Required" error. What should I do?
1. Open **System Settings** on your Mac.
2. Go to **Privacy & Security → Screen Recording**.
3. Find **TrackOwl** in the list and toggle it on.
4. Restart TrackOwl and try starting a session again.

### How do I update the desktop app?
TrackOwl updates itself. When a new version is published the app offers it to you in a dialog, and installs it once you accept. You can also download the current build again from the **Download** section of the TrackOwl website: Windows (.exe or .msi) and macOS (Apple Silicon or Intel).

### How do I cancel my subscription?
Sign in at **trackowl.io/dashboard**, open **Settings → Billing & Plans**, and follow the cancellation steps. Billing is Owner-only, so if you do not see Billing & Plans in the sidebar, ask the workspace Owner. You can also email contact@trackowl.io and we will cancel it for you within one business day.

### My screenshots are not uploading. What should I do?
Make sure you have an active internet connection during tracking. TrackOwl queues screenshots locally and syncs them when the connection is restored. If the problem persists, try signing out and back in, then contact support.

### How do I delete my account and data?
Email [contact@trackowl.io](mailto:contact@trackowl.io) with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We will permanently delete your account and all associated data within 30 days, in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

### Is my data encrypted?
Yes. All data transmitted between the TrackOwl desktop app and our servers is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2+. Data at rest is stored in encrypted databases. See our [Security & Privacy](/support/category/security-and-privacy) articles for full details.

### I found a bug. How do I report it?
Please email [contact@trackowl.io](mailto:contact@trackowl.io) with a description of the issue, your operating system and version (Windows or macOS), and any error messages you saw. Screenshots or screen recordings are always helpful.
