# Task due dates

> [!NOTE]
> A to-do in TrackOwl carries a title, a description, one or more assignees, a status and a due date. There is no time estimate field.

## Overview
The due date is how you say when a to-do is expected to be finished. It shows on the to-do and gives you something to filter and sort by when you are looking for what is late.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Who can do this:** **Owner**, **Admin**, **Manager**, **Viewer** and **Client** accounts can open To-Dos. The **User** role cannot — it sees an "Admin-only area" notice instead.

## Where to find it
1. Open the TrackOwl dashboard and sign in.
2. In the left sidebar, open **Projects**.
3. Select **To-Dos** (`/dashboard/projects/todos`).

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Create a to-do, or open one that already exists.
2. Set the **Due date** field.
3. Save. The due date appears on the to-do in the list.
4. Move the to-do through **Todo**, **In Progress** and **Done** as the work happens.

---

## Best Practices
> [!TIP]
> - Put a due date on anything you intend to chase. Without one there is nothing to sort by.
> - Use the status filter alongside due dates to see what is still open and already late.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I estimate how many hours a task will take?**
A: No. To-dos do not carry an hours estimate. If you want to compare planned against tracked effort, set a budget on the project instead — projects support budgets in hours or amount.

**Q: Can I do this from the desktop app?**
A: No. The desktop app tracks time, activity and screenshots. To-dos are managed in the dashboard in your browser.
