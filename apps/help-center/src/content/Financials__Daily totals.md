# Daily totals

> [!NOTE]
> Hello and welcome! If you are a beginner, don't worry. TrackOwl is designed to be very intuitive. This step-by-step guide covers how to use the **Daily totals** feature in the Financials section.

## Overview
Understanding how to use the **Daily totals** feature is essential for maximizing operational visibility and team productivity. This tool has been engineered to provide detailed insights while remaining incredibly user-friendly.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Required Permissions:** You must be an `Owner` or `Manager` to modify these settings. Standard users have read-only access.

## Where to find it
To access this feature:
1. Open the main **TrackOwl Dashboard**.
2. Look for the **Financials** icon in the primary sidebar navigation.
3. Select **Daily totals** from the secondary menu.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

Follow these steps to configure your settings properly:

1. Make sure you are logged into your TrackOwl workspace.
2. Look at the left-hand menu and click on **Financials**.
3. Find and click on the specific item you want to work on.
4. Make your desired changes on the screen.
5. Always remember to click **Save** or **Update** at the bottom so you don't lose your work!
