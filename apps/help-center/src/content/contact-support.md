# Contact Support

If you need help with TrackOwl, our support team is ready to assist you. You can reach us through any of the channels below.

## Email Support

For account issues, billing questions, technical problems, or any other inquiries, email us directly:

**[support@trackowl.io](mailto:support@trackowl.io)**

We aim to respond to all support emails within **24 hours** on business days (Monday–Friday, 9 AM–5 PM UTC).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is the desktop app asking for Screen Recording permission?
TrackOwl uses macOS's built-in screen capture capability to take periodic screenshots during tracked sessions. This is a core part of the time-tracking feature and requires explicit permission from macOS. You can grant it in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording**.

### The app shows a "Screen Recording Permission Required" error. What should I do?
1. Open **System Settings** on your Mac.
2. Go to **Privacy & Security → Screen Recording**.
3. Find **TrackOwl** in the list and toggle it on.
4. Restart TrackOwl and try starting a session again.

### How do I update TrackOwl on macOS (App Store version)?
The Mac App Store version of TrackOwl is updated through the Mac App Store itself. Open the **App Store**, go to the **Updates** tab, and click **Update** next to TrackOwl.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
Log in to the [TrackOwl web app](https://app.trackowl.io), navigate to **Settings → Billing**, and follow the cancellation steps. Alternatively, email us at support@trackowl.io and we will cancel it for you within one business day.

### My screenshots are not uploading. What should I do?
Make sure you have an active internet connection during tracking. TrackOwl will queue screenshots locally and sync them when the connection is restored. If the problem persists, try signing out and back in, then contact support.

### How do I delete my account and data?
Email [support@trackowl.io](mailto:support@trackowl.io) with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We will permanently delete your account and all associated data within 30 days, in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

### Is my data encrypted?
Yes. All data transmitted between the TrackOwl desktop app and our servers is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2+. Data at rest is stored in encrypted Supabase databases. See our [Security & Privacy](/category/Security%20%26%20Privacy) articles for full details.

### I found a bug. How do I report it?
Please email [support@trackowl.io](mailto:support@trackowl.io) with a description of the issue, your macOS version, and any error messages you saw. Screenshots or screen recordings are always helpful.
